## About Me

LJT is an academic researcher in the field of artificial intelligence, specializing in natural language processing (NLP) and large language models (LLMs) research. He is currently a postdoctoral researcher at Tsinghua University, working in the School of Computer Science.

## Academic Background

- **2019 - 2023**: PhD in Computer Science, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China. Thesis: "Efficient Fine-tuning and Deployment of Large Language Models for Downstream Tasks"
- **2016 - 2019**: Master of Science in Computer Science, Peking University, Beijing, China. Focus: Natural Language Processing
- **2012 - 2016**: Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China.

## Research Experience

- **2023 - Present**: Postdoctoral Researcher, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China. Research focus on efficient LLM fine-tuning, knowledge distillation, and application of LLMs in scientific research.
- **2021 - 2023**: Research Intern, Microsoft Research Asia, Beijing, China. Worked on low-resource NLP tasks and multilingual language models.
- **2020 - 2021**: Research Assistant, Tsinghua University Natural Language Processing Lab, Beijing, China. Developed dialogue systems for customer service.

## Skills

- **Programming Languages**: Python, Java, C++, JavaScript
- **Frameworks & Libraries**: PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face Transformers, scikit-learn, NumPy, Pandas
